Shader Differential Container Metrics Policy

The instruction_count and register_count metrics are read from the emitted Cg
containers. They are not recomputed from decoded instructions by the harness.

container-metrics-baseline.csv is the gate trigger. When it is present beside
stage-differential.ps1, staging reports SDIFF-METRICS-GATE and fails by default
if any row has a larger instruction_delta or register_delta than its matching
baseline row. The match key is role, name, profile, source, and uniform_set.

Use -MetricsReportOnly for explicit investigative runs that should print the
same gate line without failing the stage. New rows missing from the baseline
are reported as missing_baseline_rows and do not fail by themselves; accepting
new rows should update the baseline in the same reviewed change.

The role=reference baseline rows are cost protection for the curated durable
reference-pair stage. They are not corpus-wide cost coverage; broader corpus
cost policy lives in the reference-corpus and reference-tree-corpus roles.
As recorded on 2026-09-05, those 66 rows total 1447 instructions against the
reference's 934, and 266 registers against the reference's 152, with 45 of 66
rows carrying a positive register delta. The baseline records current cost, not
acceptable cost; baseline_regressions=0 means nothing got worse, not that the
cost is acceptable.

If every current row is missing from a present baseline, the gate reports
status=no-coverage instead of status=ok. In fail-by-default mode that fails the
stage, because no baseline row was actually compared.

Any commit that moves an existing baseline row in the worse direction must say
so in its commit message and explain why the higher cost is accepted.
